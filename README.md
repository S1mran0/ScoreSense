# #Grade Quest
A simple C program that turns your academic grades into a game.
Enter your subject scores each term and the program tells you your trend, predicted next grade, 
how many hours to study,and whether you defeated the Final Boss.

## How to Play
1. Enter how many subjects you have
2. Enter how many rounds (terms) of grades you have
3. Enter each subject name and its grades per term
4. The program shows your Battle Report for each subject
5. At the end, face the Final Boss based on your overall average
